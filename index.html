Welcome to Velvet

A Sanctuary of Flavor & Relaxation

Born from a passion for the art of hookah, Velvet Lounge opened its doors to create a space where tradition and modern luxury coexist. Every detail — from the hand-selected tobaccos to the ambient lighting — has been curated to awaken your senses.

Our philosophy is simple: slow down, savour the moment, and let the world outside fade away. Whether you're a seasoned enthusiast or exploring hookah for the first time, our expert mixologists craft each session to your personal taste.

With plush velvet seating, curated music, and an ever-evolving menu of premium blends, Velvet Lounge is more than a destination — it's a ritual.
