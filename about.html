Our Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Velvet

Our relentless pursuit of perfection defines every session, flavor, and moment spent within our walls.

Artisanal Leaf Selection

We exclusively source golden Virginia and dark-leaf tobaccos cured with cold-pressed botanical essences, natural cane molasses, and premium fruit extracts.

Precision Thermal Control

Using aerospace-grade aluminum heat management devices and zero-chemical coconut coals, we guarantee a pure, velvety draw without harsh burning.

Curated Flavor Mixology

Each session is complemented by craft mocktails, Moroccan mint teas, and Turkish coffees designed by sommeliers to accentuate every aromatic note.

The Ritual

The Art of the Perfect Session

Four deliberate steps masterfully executed by our certified shisha mixologists.

01

Leaf Aeration & Fluffing

Tobaccos are delicately fluffed and separated by hand to ensure even thermal circulation and prevent hot spots inside the clay phunnel bowl.

02

Crystal Base Infusion

Pure mountain spring water is paired with crushed mint leaves, fresh citrus wheels, or sub-zero ice cylinders for a brisk, cooling inhale.

03

Coal Ignition & Calibrating

Organic coconut coals are heated to an even crimson glow, rested in an airflow-controlled chamber, and pre-warmed for 3 minutes.

04

The Velvet Exhale

Delivered to your lounge table with single-use hygienic silicone wands, ready to produce hours of rich, billowing, aromatic smoke.
