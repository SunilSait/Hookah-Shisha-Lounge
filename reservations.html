Policies

Reservation Policies

Reservations can be cancelled or modified free of charge up to 2 hours before your scheduled time. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a $25 fee for standard bookings and $50 for VIP suites.

We hold reservations for 15 minutes past the scheduled time. After 15 minutes, your table may be released to walk-in guests. Please call us if you're running late and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

All guests must be at least 21 years of age. Valid government-issued photo ID is required at the door. We cannot make exceptions to this policy.

Smart casual attire is recommended. We reserve the right to deny entry to guests wearing athletic wear, flip-flops, or excessively casual clothing. Themed event nights may have specific dress code requirements.
